About

As a student at Code the Dream, I combine a professional background in metrology with the perspective of someone who cares a great deal about how things look and feel.

That mix means I approach technology with two habits at once. Extreme precision, because a calibration that is off by a fraction invalidates everything downstream. And a willingness to try the unobvious approach. I am persistent by temperament: I would rather sit with a problem until it gives way than hand it to someone else.

Outside of coursework I keep building. Cloud infrastructure, private VPNs, home automation, and small security tools. Integrating AI and emerging tooling into that daily practice is how I learn fastest, and how I turn textbook concepts into things that actually run.

Experience

Where the precision comes from.

  1. Metrology and ISO standards

    Calibration, traceability, and hands-on implementation of ISO/IEC 17025, the standard that decides whether a laboratory’s results can be trusted at all. Precision here is not a preference, it is the deliverable.

  2. Infrastructure and security

    Deployed personal cloud servers and private VPNs from scratch, which taught me networking, certificate handling, and data protection the slow way, by breaking them and fixing them.

  3. AI and automation

    Ongoing automated cybersecurity projects that use AI and machine learning to keep an environment observable and secure. This is the practical path I am taking toward security engineering.

  4. Persistence and problem solving

    A self-starter who researches, troubleshoots, and finishes complex technical projects without being handed the answer. Most of what I know came from a project that refused to work.
